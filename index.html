Good to know

Frequently asked.

Can I add more than one vehicle to a trip?

Yes. Add multiple rides and configure quantity and rental days independently before reviewing the total.

Does sharing my location change vehicles or prices?

No. Location is only displayed as a convenience. The fleet, ordering and prices stay the same.

Can I search with incomplete or misspelled names?

Yes. Search suggestions update on every character and tolerate common typing mistakes such as “swft” for Swift.

How long can I set a rental for?

Each vehicle can be configured from 1 to 30 rental days in this release.

Where can I see recent trip confirmations?

Open your profile or use Trip history in the footer to revisit confirmations saved in this browser.
