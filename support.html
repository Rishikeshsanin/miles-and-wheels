Frequently asked

Good to know.

Can I add more than one vehicle to a trip?

Yes. Add multiple rides, set whole-number quantities and configure rental days independently before review.

Can I compare vehicles?

Yes. On the Fleet page, select up to three rides and compare price, fuel, transmission, capacity, included distance and deposit side by side.

Does the search handle spelling mistakes?

Yes. Search updates on every character and uses close-match scoring for common typing mistakes.

What does the location control do?

It shows your current location in the top utility bar so your trip context stays visible while you browse.

Where can I see recent trips?

Open Profile from the header and choose Trip history, or use the Trip history link in the footer.

Terms

Current release.

Online payment, live inventory reservation and identity verification are not connected yet. The current release focuses on the complete discovery, comparison, trip-building and confirmation experience before those operational systems are connected.

Privacy

Minimal by design.

A display name, favourites, trip selections and recent trip history are kept on the current device for continuity. Location is requested only when you choose to add it. No account database is connected yet.
