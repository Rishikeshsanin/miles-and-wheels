Frequently asked

Good to know.

Can I add more than one vehicle to a trip?

Yes. Add multiple rides, set whole-number quantities and configure rental days independently before review.

Can I compare vehicles?

Yes. On the Fleet page, select up to three rides and compare price, fuel, transmission, capacity, included distance and deposit side by side.

Does the search handle spelling mistakes?

Yes. Search updates on every character and uses close-match scoring for common typing mistakes.

What does the location control do?

It keeps your current location visible in the utility dock while you browse.

Where can I see recent trips?

Open Profile from the header and choose Trip history, or use the Trip history link in the footer.

Does the site support dark mode?

Yes. Use the theme control in the header. Your preference is remembered on this browser.

Terms

Booking scope.

Trip confirmation currently does not charge a payment method or reserve live fleet inventory. Those operational systems will be connected after the customer experience and frontend are finalized.

Privacy

Minimal by design.

A display name, favourites, trip selections and recent trip history stay on the current browser for continuity. Location is requested only when you choose to add it.
